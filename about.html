Why FuseZap exists

The hard part is everything after the chip.

Building a chip is years of hard work. Then comes a second hard part: getting the models people actually want to run working on it.

Customers expect the newest open models, the APIs they already use, and results they can trust. Building all of that takes time chip makers would rather spend on their hardware.

That’s the gap we fill. FuseZap gives chip makers a complete way to serve models, built on top of the stack they already have.

A note from Promod

Why we built FuseZap.

A chip takes years. Thousands of decisions, a board on the bench, and the day the first silicon comes back and works. It is an extraordinary engineering achievement. But hardware alone doesn’t make a platform successful.

The real challenge begins when developers try to run the models they actually care about. A new accelerator can have impressive specifications and still sit unused, because the software isn’t ready. Models evolve, frameworks change, and every release is another chance for something to break.

We know that feeling from the inside. We brought up AWS Inferentia2, a chip we didn’t design, on a simulator first. On the first full run on the real chip, 1,171 of 1,174 tests passed. We remember the three that didn’t more than all the ones that did.

That’s why we’re building FuseZap. FuseZap Core helps you get models running on your chip. FuseZap Validate helps you keep them working, release after release.

Because the true measure of an AI platform isn’t just what its silicon is capable of.

It’s what developers can build with it.

Promod GangadharanCo-Founder and CEO, Techyodl
